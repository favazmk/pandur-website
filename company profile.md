# PANDUR COMPANY PROFILE

**Royal Quality Bakes LLC — Zubara, Khorfakkan, UAE**

---

## Company Introduction

**PANDUR**  
*Our Signature Taste. Made to Grow.*

Pandur is the signature cookie brand of Royal Quality Bakes LLC, backed by 45 years of experience in the food manufacturing and bakery industry.

Based in Zubara, Khorfakkan, Pandur combines traditional bakery expertise with modern food manufacturing and packaging capabilities to create products that deliver excellent taste, consistent quality, and reliable shelf life.

Our experience in the industry has given us a strong understanding of consumer preferences, product quality, production efficiency, and market requirements. With Pandur, we are bringing that experience into a new generation of bakery products designed for today’s UAE and GCC markets.

---

Our growing distribution presence provides a strong foundation for expanding Pandur across the wider UAE and, ultimately, throughout the GCC region.

## About Pandur

Pandur is more than a cookie — it is our signature product.

We developed Pandur with a clear objective: to offer consumers a delicious, high-quality cookie with a distinctive taste and dependable shelf life.

With a six-month shelf life, Pandur is designed to meet the practical requirements of modern retail, distribution, and supermarket channels while maintaining its taste and quality.

We have currently launched four signature flavours, carefully developed to offer consumers different taste experiences and to compete confidently within the existing cookie market.

Our ambition is to establish Pandur as a trusted UAE bakery brand and expand its presence across the GCC.
